Hi, I'm Chris Ge. I've recently finished my Bachelor's in CS (course 6-3) at MIT, and I'm now pursuing a 4th year MEng in CS. My research interest is in using interpretability to help understand and control the models people actually use.

At MIT, I'm a part of [Antonio Torralba Lab](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/vision/torralbalab/), where I do mechanistic interpretability research on models of practical interest, including multimodal diffusion transformer models like FLUX.2. I also have industry experience with agents: at [Fulcrum](https://fulcrum.inc/), I trained models to predict how likely an agent is to succeed on a given task, and at [Hudson River Trading](https://www.hudsonrivertrading.com/) (research intern on HRT AI Labs), I worked on AI agent autoresearch setups for model development. Outside the lab, I'm a TA for [Introduction to Machine Learning (6.3900)](https://introml.mit.edu/spring26) at MIT, where I teach a recitation section and help develop course content.
