Hi, I'm Chris Ge. I've recently finished my Bachelor's in CS (course 6-3) at MIT, and I'm now pursuing a 4th year MEng in CS. My research interest is in using interpretability to help understand and control the models people actually use, particularly for AI safety applications.

At MIT, I'm (remotely) advised by [Tamar Rott Shaham](https://tamarott.github.io/), and I'm also currently working together with [David Bau](https://baulab.info/) on training models to identify and explain the salient details of an example or a family of examples. Previously, I've used mechanistic interpretability to investigate vision-language binding in image-editing models. I also have industry experience with agents: at [Fulcrum](https://fulcrum.inc/), I trained models to predict how likely an agent is to succeed on a given task, and at [Hudson River Trading](https://www.hudsonrivertrading.com/) (research intern on HRT AI Labs), I worked on AI agent autoresearch setups for model development. Outside the lab, I'm a TA for [Introduction to Machine Learning (6.3900)](https://introml.mit.edu/spring26) at MIT, where I teach a recitation section and help develop course content.
